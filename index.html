How it works

Three steps. Ten minutes. Then it runs itself.

You add the local pros you already trust. You share your page once. After that, Hootova writes the emails, sends them under your name, pays for the magnets and the flyers, checks in with your clients after every job, and pays you every month.

What you already do

You already know who's actually good. Your clients get treated better because of it.

You know who does the work right, not who has the most reviews. You know what a job should cost, so your client doesn't collect three quotes and get talked into extras they didn't need. You know who actually shows up.

And your name on the job changes how your client gets treated. When a painter or a plumber hears the work came from you, they show up on time and they quote fair, because your next call is worth more to them than any single invoice.

You've been running that service for years. You've just never been paid for it, and it stops the day the sale closes.

When I moved from Massachusetts to North Carolina, my car's A/C went out. I called three mechanics and got three different diagnoses. The cheapest quote was $2,500.

Then I called a local realtor for the name of someone she trusted. One phone call later, the actual problem (a leak) cost $400 to find, patch, and refill.

That's the power of a local connection. It's why I built Hootova.

Why agents use it

What Hootova does while you're out working.

Stay top of mind

Your clients get regular emails with local offers and seasonal reminders, written by Hootova and sent under your name. You stay in their inbox all year without writing a thing.

See what it's actually worth

Lead totals, page traffic, which local pros your clients contacted, and when. Those businesses see who sent them the work. You finally see what your recommendations produce.

Keep the work good

Hootova checks in with your clients after every job. If something goes wrong, you hear about it first, and you can take any business off your page at any time.

And yes, the money part

You're paid for reach, the way an influencer is. Never per job.

Hootova pays you every month based on how many people use your page, not on what any one business makes from it. That distinction is the whole thing. The moment you'd earn more by steering a client toward one particular painter, your recommendation stops being a recommendation and your clients would be right to stop trusting it. So you're paid for the size of your influence and for keeping a list worth trusting, which is exactly what you were doing for free.

No kickbacks

You're paid for reach and for the quality of your list, never for an individual job or lead. That's what keeps this clear of settlement-services rules.

Fully disclosed

Every page tells your clients you're compensated, in plain language. You don't write it and you don't add it. It's already there.

Everyday trades only

Landscapers, mechanics, electricians, plumbers, painters, house cleaners, photographers. Nothing tied to a closing, ever.

Your page, your standards

You decide who goes on it and you can take anyone off at any time. We pay you to keep the list good, not to push anybody.

"I'm compensated by Hootova based on traffic to my page, which is how I make sure these businesses take great care of the people I send them. If your experience is anything less than excellent, let me know. I'll handle it."

This is the disclosure your clients see. It's already written and already on your page.
